About Shree Sandwich: Wild Fermentation

Greatness takes time. Our signature sourdough isn't rushed. It's born from a carefully nurtured starter and allowed a slow, 48-hour fermentation process. This patience yields a complex, tangy flavor profile and an airy, structured crumb that holds up perfectly to our hearty fillings.
